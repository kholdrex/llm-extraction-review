# Third-party data

`results/*.jsonl` and `data/sample.json` contain requests and identifiers from the MASSIVE dataset 1.1
(https://github.com/alexa/massive), © Amazon.com, Inc., licensed under CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). According to its NOTICE file, the English portion of MASSIVE is
derived from SLURP (https://github.com/pswietojanski/slurp), also under CC BY 4.0. The MIT licence of this
repository covers the code only.

`results/replication/` and `data/replication_manifest.json` contain requests and identifiers from MASSIVE (above) and
from the SNIPS benchmark of June 2017 by Snips (https://github.com/sonos/nlu-benchmark), released under CC0 1.0
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) with a request to cite A. Coucke et al., "Snips Voice Platform:
an embedded Spoken Language Understanding system for private-by-design voice interfaces", 2018
(https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.10190). The SNIPS requests are taken from the tagged train/valid/test split distributed
with C.-W. Goo et al., "Slot-Gated Modeling for Joint Slot Filling and Intent Prediction", NAACL 2018
(https://github.com/MiuLab/SlotGated-SLU), which states no licence of its own; that split is downloaded at a pinned
commit and not redistributed here.
